# Every control under 44 under a coarse pointer, by screen

The test database (seventeen records), desktop Chromium with touch on, at 390 and 1280. “Under 44” is a control whose own box is under 44 tall and that a tap reaches over fewer than 44 rows of pixels down its middle. The header is its own row and is left out of each screen’s. The counts are the fixtures’; the kinds are the app’s. Not here: the shared dialog (step 128, 3 of 3), look up’s results (step 125: five controls a card, held), the shelf’s records themselves, which are drawn on a canvas and are not controls a reader of the page can find, and the pages under /wall/probe, which are tools.
