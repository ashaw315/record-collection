# Every control under 44 under a coarse pointer, by screen: down, and across

The test database (seventeen records), desktop Chromium with touch on, at 390 and 1280. “Under 44 down” is a control whose own box is under 44 tall and that a tap reaches over fewer than 44 rows of pixels down its middle. “Under 44 across” is one a tap reaches over fewer than 44 pixels across its middle, its drawn width and whatever reaches it from either side. The header is its own row and is left out of each screen’s. The counts are the fixtures’; the kinds are the app’s. Not here: the shared dialog (step 128, 3 of 3 down), look up’s results (step 125: five controls a card, held) and the pages under /wall/probe, which are tools.
