P.2 · What an ask does

The moods toggle, and a separate ASK asks. Pressing a mood selects or clears it and asks nothing. Pressing ASK asks, and pressing it again asks again. A mood that asked when pressed would make choosing two moods impossible, and a second press on SLEEPY would mean either ask again or clear it. ASK is inert while no mood is selected. This is Adam’s.

The ask leans; it does not filter. Each record in the pool is scored by how many of the selected moods it carries, and the pick is drawn at random from the highest score. A record carrying none of them is never drawn. So an ask has an answer whenever any record in the pool carries any selected mood, at two moods or at eight. Adding a mood can still shrink the highest score’s tier, where some records carry both, so the lean is not a widening either. It only never empties. Nothing on the control may imply narrowing: no count beside a mood, no ALL or AND, no figure that changes as moods are added.

The same record is not served twice in a row while another remains. The page holds one last-served record, set by every answer, ASK’s and SURPRISE ME’s alike. The pick excludes it, unless excluding it would leave nothing, when it draws from the whole tier and may serve it again. A record the reader pulled by its spine counts as served too, since the annoyance Adam names is the same record twice in a row, and pulling one, asking, and getting it back is that. The rule is Adam’s; the spine clause is mine, from his reason.

The pool is what the screen shows. Under a filter or a search, an ask draws from the matches. The answer arrives on the screen the reader is on, and a record that screen has hidden would be pulled from a seat the filter emptied, or shown beside a grid that left it out. The moods themselves change nothing on the screen: no seat empties, no row or cover hides, and the count does not move.

It is not a filter in any other respect. The selected moods and the last-served record are held by the page only. Nothing goes to the address, a stored preference or the Sort control, and a navigation clears them. This is Adam’s.

SURPRISE ME draws from the whole pool, tagged or not, ignoring the moods and leaving them selected, under the same last-served rule.

No entry: nothing was ruled here before. What is new is the ask, the lean, the last-served rule and the pool.

P.6 · The states

A mood is inert when no tagged record carries it. It stays drawn at its size in the label colour, cannot be selected, and is named as unavailable to assistive technology. Inertness is counted over the whole collection, not over what a filter leaves, so a filter never changes the control.

Whether an inert mood is empty or waiting is a fact about the collection, not about the mood. A record not yet tagged could carry any mood, so while any record is untagged no mood can honestly be called empty. So the difference is drawn once, for the collection. While any record is untagged, a line beneath the moods gives how many are not yet tagged, and every inert mood reads under it as possibly waiting. With every record tagged the line is absent, and an inert mood is empty. Some tagged and some not is the normal state between adding a record and running the script. The line is drawn when the page loads, never in answer to a press, so it moves nothing a reader is about to press.

An untagged record is reached only by SURPRISE ME, which ignores tags, so no record is out of reach while the script has not run.

With nothing tagged, the control is SURPRISE ME alone, with no moods, no ASK and no line. Eight words that cannot be pressed would be a control drawn to be refused. The form stands for a collection with no tags. Withdrawn within §P.6: Phase 0 building the control in its nothing-tagged form, with the moods joining when the first tags land. The tags landed on 24 October, with all 22 records tagged, so that state is unreachable on the real collection, and a control gated on tags being absent would vanish on load. It was carried at 5c821c3, so the entry is owed. Phase 0 builds the permanent control: SURPRISE ME, the reserved slot, and the place the moods and ASK will take, with the moods not yet drawn. Nothing in it is gated on tags being absent. The moods and ASK join in their own step, and from then the nothing-tagged form is drawn only where the collection has no tags.

An ask the pool cannot answer draws nothing. Where the selected moods have records but none among what the screen shows, there is no pull and no cell, and the slot says nothing showing carries these moods.

The slot’s messages are written to fit it. The slot is §T.6’s reserved slot, and its rule that a message is rewritten rather than cut reaches the rail, where one line is narrow. Proposed: “The only one.”, “None showing.”, and for the line, “4 not tagged yet.” The step checks each at the rail’s width and at 320 and 1280.

Reduced motion is §W.8’s. Under it the wall moves nothing on its own, so a re-roll’s put back and pull both cut, and the next record is simply out. On the table and the grid an answer never moves. Nothing is ruled here.

No entry: nothing was ruled here before. What is new is the inert mood, the untagged line, the surprise-only form and the unanswerable ask.
