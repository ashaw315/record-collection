The weights are the settled file’s drawing, not its prose: 500 for the wordmark and the current link, 400 for the others. No withdrawal is owed for this section. It builds three settled rulings and changes none: §4.1’s type for the header, §3’s current mark, and §4.1’s .10em across the four areas the shared style reaches. The tracking paragraphs give the reasons for §4.1 and §24.
